Teaching

Courses and educational resources by Ricardo Custódio.

Teaching has been an integral part of my academic career.

My courses are closely connected to my research in information security, cryptography, digital identity, electronic documents, artificial intelligence, and the mathematical foundations of computer science.

This section also serves as a long-term archive of lecture notes, slides, exercises, assignments, and other educational resources developed throughout my academic career.

Current Courses

Mathematical Foundations for Computer Science

Topics include mathematical reasoning, logic, proofs, induction, recurrences, combinatorics, relations, functions, and other mathematical foundations used in computer science.

Teaching Philosophy

I view teaching and research as closely connected activities.

Students should not only learn established concepts and technologies, but also understand the problems that motivated them, their limitations, and the open questions that remain.

In information security and cryptography in particular, this means combining theoretical foundations with the analysis and construction of real systems.
