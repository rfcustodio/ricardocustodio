Supervision

My academic supervision has focused primarily on cryptography, information security, digital identity, digital signatures, electronic documents, and related areas of computer science.

Over the years, I have supervised undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral students, many of whom have continued their careers in academia, research, industry, and technology development.

Current doctoral and master’s students are listed below. Research topics reflect the structured supervision records maintained for this website.

Academic Genealogy

Academic supervision is more than a list of theses and dissertations. It represents the development of research lines, collaborations, and successive generations of researchers.

Several students initially supervised at the master’s level later continued their doctoral studies, while former students have themselves become researchers, professors, and supervisors.

A structured academic genealogy connecting students, publications, projects, software, and research topics is under development.
