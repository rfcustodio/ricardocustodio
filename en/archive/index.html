Academic Archive

Keywords

cryptography, information security, digital trust, digital signatures, public-key infrastructure, electronic identity, electronic documents, post-quantum cryptography, finite fields, artificial intelligence security

My academic work has developed across engineering, scientific computing, cryptography, electronic documents, digital signatures, identity, post-quantum cryptography, and artificial intelligence.

This archive is not intended to reproduce a curriculum vitae. It is a historical view of how research questions, projects, technological artifacts, publications, students, and collaborations have evolved and intersected over time.
