Publications

Scientific publications by Ricardo Custódio.

My research publications span information security, cryptography, digital signatures, digital identity, electronic documents, finite-field arithmetic, post-quantum cryptography, and artificial intelligence applied to security.

This bibliography is being progressively consolidated from DBLP, ORCID, Lattes, Google Scholar, and my personal academic archive.

Research Areas

Publications can broadly be associated with the following research directions:

  • Cryptography and cryptographic protocols
  • Finite-field arithmetic
  • Digital signatures
  • Public key infrastructure
  • Digital identity
  • Electronic documents and preservation
  • Post-quantum cryptography
  • Artificial intelligence and security
